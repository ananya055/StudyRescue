# StudyRescue
A Study Planner to Manage Subjects, Track Tasks, and Monitor Study Progress.

About the Project

StudyRescue is a simple web application I created to make studying and managing academic work easier. It helps students keep their subjects, study topics and exam dates organized in one place. Instead of keeping track of everything separately, students can use one dashboard to plan their studies and see how much they have completed.

Feature
- **Dashboard:** Shows the total number of subjects, pending topics, completed topics and overall progress.
- **Manage Subjects:** Add new subjects, edit existing ones or delete subjects you no longer need.
- **Built-in Subjects:** Comes with predefined subjects like C, Python, Java, DBMS, Data Structures, Web Development and more.
- **Study Planner:** Add study topics, choose a date and set their priority as Normal, Important or High.
- **Manage Study Tasks:** Mark topics as completed, delete them or change their study dates.
- **Task Filters:** Easily switch between all, pending, completed and missed topics.
- **Exam Tracker:** Add upcoming exams and see how many days are left.
- **Progress Tracking:** Check your completed topics and overall progress through dashboard statistics and a progress bar.
- **Data Saving:** Keeps your subjects, tasks and exam details saved using browser Local Storage.

How to Use
1. Open the website and start from the dashboard.
2. Go to **My Subjects** to explore the available subjects or add your own.
3. Add and manage the subjects you want to study.
4. Mark topics as completed whenever you finish them.
5. Use the **Study Planner** to add topics along with their study dates and priorities.
6. Filter your topics to see what is pending, completed or missed.
7. Change the date of a topic if you need more time to complete it.
8. Add your exam dates to keep track of upcoming exams.
9. Visit **My Progress** to see how much of your study work is completed.

How StudyRescue Helps Students
- Keeps all study-related information in one place.
- Makes it easier to plan what to study each day.
- Helps students remember pending topics and important tasks.
- Makes it easier to keep track of missed study dates.
- Helps students prepare for exams by keeping exam dates visible.
- Shows how much work has been completed and what is left.
- Encourages students to follow a regular study routine.
- Saves time by keeping everything organized in one place.

Technologies Used
- **HTML5** – Used to create the structure of the website.
- **CSS3** – Used for the design, layout and responsive styling.
- **JavaScript** – Used to handle the features and user interactions.
- **Local Storage** – Used to save study data in the browser.

Why I Created This Project
I created StudyRescue to make everyday study planning a little easier. Managing different subjects, remembering unfinished topics and keeping track of exam dates can sometimes become confusing. I wanted to bring these things together in one place and make it easier for students Lik me to stay organized and keep moving forward.
